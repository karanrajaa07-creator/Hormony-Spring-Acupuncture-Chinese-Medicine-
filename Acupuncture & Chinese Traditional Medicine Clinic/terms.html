Clinic Policies

Terms & Conditions

Please review our treatment policies, appointment scheduling terms, and medical disclaimers.

1. 24-Hour Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

When you schedule an appointment, that clinical room and practitioner time is reserved exclusively for you. We kindly require at least 24 hours advance notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment through your Patient Dashboard or by calling our clinic. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice or missed appointments may be subject to a missed appointment fee.

2. Medical Scope of Practice Disclaimer

Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), acupuncture, moxibustion, cupping, and herbal medicine are designed to stimulate your body’s innate healing capacity and promote holistic physiological balance. TCM care is complementary to conventional Western medicine and does not replace emergency medical care or hospital triage. We make no representations or warranties of guaranteed cures. Always consult your primary care physician for acute or life-threatening symptoms.

3. Patient Disclosure Responsibilities

For your clinical safety, patients agree to provide accurate, up-to-date medical history and immediately notify their practitioner of:

  • Pregnancy or suspected pregnancy (specific acupoints and herbs are contraindicated during pregnancy).
  • Cardiac pacemakers or implanted electronic medical devices (contraindication for electro-acupuncture).
  • Bleeding disorders or current use of prescription blood thinners (anticoagulants).
  • All prescription medications and over-the-counter pharmaceuticals to prevent botanical herb-drug interactions.
  • Any documented allergies to botanicals, pollen, or latex.

4. Professional Sterile Technique Standards

In accordance with California state licensing requirements and Clean Needle Technique (CNT) protocols, we use exclusively pre-sterilized, single-use, surgical-grade stainless steel needles. Needles are individually packaged, opened at point of use, and discarded in medical sharps biohazard containers immediately following treatment.

5. Herbal Dispensary Terms

All dispensed botanical formulas are custom-compounded for the individual patient based on direct tongue and pulse diagnostic patterns. Herbal formulas are strictly non-transferable and should not be shared with others. Concentrated granules should be reconstituted and consumed strictly in accordance with written instructions displayed in your Patient Dashboard.
