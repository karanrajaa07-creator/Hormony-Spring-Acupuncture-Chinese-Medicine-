Our Heritage & Vision

Ancient Wisdom Rooted in Compassionate Modern Care

Harmony Spring was founded with a clear purpose: to create a tranquil sanctuary where patients are never rushed, where root imbalances are addressed, and where Traditional Chinese Medicine is practiced with the highest clinical integrity.

Our Origin

Bridging Classical Canons and Contemporary Health

Established in 2010 by Dr. Meilin Chen and Dr. Jonathan Wu, Harmony Spring Acupuncture & TCM Clinic emerged from a shared observation: modern urban life places relentless demands on the human nervous system, often resulting in fragmented, symptom-chasing treatments.

Drawing upon classical works such as the Huangdi Neijing (The Yellow Emperor's Classic of Medicine) and the Shanghan Lun, we treat each patient as a complete, living ecosystem. We understand that a chronic tension headache may stem from emotional liver constraint, or that digestive fatigue may reflect an exhausted spleen meridian.

By uniting gentle meridian acupuncture with clean, laboratory-tested herbal extracts, we empower our patients to experience lasting homeostasis and self-healing.

Theoretical Foundations

The Five Elements (*Wu Xing*) Framework

In Traditional Chinese Medicine, nature and human physiology are organized around five elemental phases that generate and regulate each other.

木 • Wood

Liver & Gallbladder

Governs smooth Qi flow, tendons, eyes, and emotional flexibility. Spring season.

火 • Fire

Heart & Small Intestine

Governs blood vessels, joyful spirit (Shen), and mental clarity. Summer season.

土 • Earth

Spleen & Stomach

Governs digestive fire, transformation of food into Qi, and muscles. Late Summer season.

金 • Metal

Lung & Large Intestine

Governs defensive Qi (Wei Qi), respiration, skin, and letting go. Autumn season.

水 • Water

Kidney & Urinary Bladder

Governs core essence (Jing), bones, endocrine vitality, and resilience. Winter season.

Our Safety Promise

Clinical Standards That Put Your Peace of Mind First

We adhere strictly to California Acupuncture Board regulations and national OSHA healthcare protocols.

100% Sterile & Disposable

Every needle is factory-sealed, single-use, surgical-grade stainless steel. Needles are opened immediately before insertion and discarded in designated sharps biohazard containers.

GMP-Certified Botanicals

Our in-house herbal pharmacy dispenses only concentrated botanical granules independently verified for heavy metals, microbial safety, and absence of agricultural pesticides.

Confidential Health Records

All clinical diagnostic notes, tongue evaluations, and herbal prescriptions are stored in our secure, HIPAA-compliant patient management system, accessible directly through your portal.
